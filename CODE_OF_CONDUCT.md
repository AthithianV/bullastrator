# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Bullastrator is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in the project.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intentions
- Give and receive constructive feedback
- Focus on the technical issue rather than the person
- Respect different opinions and approaches
- Help maintain a welcoming environment

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberate disruption
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances
- Publishing private information without permission
- Threats or intimidation
- Any other behavior that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including warnings, removal of contributions, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Project-related communication
- Other spaces where someone is representing the Bullastrator project
